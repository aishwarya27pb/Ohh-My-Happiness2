## 2. Build Your Own Box (BYOB)
The BYOB feature is our signature interactive hamper customizer:
- **Step 1: Choose Your Box:** Select a base gift box size (Standard or Premium Leatherette) and dynamic color (Midnight Black or Royal Teal).
- **Step 2: Choose Your Fillers:** Add from over 40+ curated products, including organic teas, dry fruits, hand-poured soy candles, executive pens, and gourmet treats.
- **Step 3: Add a Card:** Customize a premium card with a hand-written personal message for the recipient.
- **Pricing Logic:** The box price dynamically updates as fillers are added. Standard packaging starts at ₹250, and premium leatherette packaging starts at ₹550.
